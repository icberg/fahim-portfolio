    Redesign Northside Block before the city does

    The municipality plans to redevelop these asphalt lots. You are a resident and a co-designer. Try ideas in the digital twin at no cost and no risk, then send the neighbourhood's proposal to the planners.

    1. Inspect a lot to see what residents need there.
    2. Place, rotate and combine modular objects on the grey lots.
    3. Watch the twin respond in heat, storm runoff, walkability and how five resident groups feel.

    Sound is on. The speaker button mutes it.

    How to play

    Controls and loops

    Controls

    • Place: pick an object, then click a grey lot. With a mouse you can also drag it from the list onto the map.
    • Rotate: press R or the Rotate button.
    • Move: with Inspect active, drag an object to another free lot.
    • Remove: use the Remove tool. Your own objects are refunded in full.
    • Camera: drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
    • Keyboard: arrow keys move across lots, Enter acts, 1 to 8 pick objects, Esc returns to Inspect.

    Three loops

    • Core: inspect, place, read the twin, earn points and citizen reactions, refine.
    • Secondary: pass three challenges on a budget, unlock advanced objects, find combos, review neighbours' designs.
    • Tertiary: earn civic badges, vote on the best design and send the report to the city planners.

    The twin is a proposal space. Planners make the final decision.
